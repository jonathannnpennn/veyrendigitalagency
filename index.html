How we use the TikTok Marketing API

Our app, VEYREN Ads Manager, is an internal tool. It only works on ad accounts whose owners have authorized us.

  • Read advertiser account details after the owner grants access.
  • Create and edit campaigns, ad groups and ads.
  • Upload and manage ad creatives.
  • Sync product catalogs for e-commerce ads.
  • Pull performance reports for our clients.
  • We never sell advertiser data or share it with third parties.
