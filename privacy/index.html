Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

VEYREN Digital Agency is a digital marketing agency based in Indonesia. This policy explains how our app, VEYREN Ads Manager, handles data.

What we collect

When an advertiser authorizes our app through TikTok, we receive access to the advertiser account data they approve: account details, campaigns, ads, creatives, catalogs and performance reports.

How we use it

We use this data only to manage advertising and create reports for the advertiser who authorized us. We do not sell data and we do not share it with third parties, except where TikTok or the law requires.

Storage and security

We keep access tokens and account data protected, and only for as long as needed to provide our service.

Your choices

An advertiser can revoke our access at any time in their TikTok Ads Manager account. You can also ask us to delete your data by email.
